Some questions must be answered as to the creation of this.

# What is the Compendium of the Mind

The compendium of the mind is a collection, an album, an anthology, a digest, a synopsis, a handbook, a manual, a precis, a treasury, an omnibus, an ana, a library, and whatever have you within [Merriam-Webster's Thesaurus](https://www.merriam-webster.com/thesaurus/compendium) or [Thesaraus.com](https://www.thesaurus.com/browse/compendium) for that is really what this is. A *something* that fits when *it* needs to be.


# Where is the Compendium of the Mind coming from

The compendium comes from **my** mind. To be more specific, it is what my mind comes up with. Not necessarily that I am making ~~shit~~ stuff up, rather it is what my mind has thought to study. However, it is not limited to what I study, but also to what I am doing. This could include a dissertation of why a website looks bad and the creative choices that can be made better, this could also include date and time logs of what I have been programming, and it could also include studying a certain programming language and its quirks.


# Why make the Compendium of the Mind

I realized that I am not getting any younger. I do not remember as many things things as I was able to remember when I was younger. Besides that, I do not have all the paper in the world nor the storage for it, nor the eligible handwriting to be able to write everything down that I want to on paper. While I could, and I am with ONE specific thing (*Its a cookbook that I want to hand down to my offspring*), its something I am taking my time on, not a write one and done. I need to be able to do a write one and done, which makes it easier to do on a keyboard, and I get to remember it just by going here!


# Who is the Compendium of the Mind for

While I am the primary stakeholder of this, this is also good to have for my fellow learners, enthusiasts, students, masters, I do not want to look for another thesaurus entry so please just do it on your own. They get to also have this when I show it to them. They can come back to this should they think I have the answer they are looking for. It is for everyone for knowledge is not something to keep.


# When will the Compendium of the Mind finish

Never. When I pass, probably.
